Policy

The spending leash and your assumptions

Every bid plan passes a policy gate before anything is shown as allowed, and every all-in total rests on two things a lot page cannot tell the agent: your tax rate and what a pickup costs you.

The gate, in order

  1. A no-bid or a hold never spends. Only a BID verdict reaches the gate.
  2. Per-lot cap. An all-in total over is denied outright. That is PropertyRoom's card and PayPal ceiling; above it only a wire works, and the agent never wires money.
  3. Session budget. What is already committed plus the new all-in may not pass .
  4. Dry-run. What passes is printed as the bid it would place, and stops there. This UI has no live mode.

A proxy bid is judged at its maximum, not at the next increment, and its maximum is sized to fit what the gate has left.

The cap, the session budget and the reserve setting come from config.json.

Your assumptions

Changing these re-works every verdict on the Lots page without calling the model. They last until you stop the app; put them in config.json to keep them.
